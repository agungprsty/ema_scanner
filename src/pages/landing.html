Methodology

How the strategy works

Every closed candle is checked for a Supertrend flip, confirmed by trend (EMA 50) and momentum (RSI).

1

Candle Close Scan

Each candle close across your configured watchlist triggers a single-flight scan against the Binance futures API.

2

Confirmed Flip, Limit Entry

A Supertrend flip only counts when EMA 50 and RSI agree with it. Then a limit entry is set at the low (long) or high (short) of that candle.

3

Automated Lifecycle

Positions track fill confirmation, fixed 2.0% TP and 1.0% SL after fees (a true 1:2), and max holding periods, with instant alerts sent via Telegram.

Entry Rules

Three checks before every signal

Supertrend (ATR 10, factor 3) finds the turn, EMA 50 confirms the trend and RSI (14) confirms the momentum without chasing an overstretched move.

LONG all three on one closed candle
  • Supertrend turns green on the candle
  • The candle closes above EMA 50
  • RSI is above 50 and has not reached 80
SHORT all three on one closed candle
  • Supertrend turns red on the candle
  • The candle closes below EMA 50
  • RSI is below 50 and has not reached 20

A flip that fails any check is skipped. If Supertrend turns green while the price is still below EMA 50, or RSI is already above 80, no signal is sent.
