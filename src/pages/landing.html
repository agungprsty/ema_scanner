Methodology

How the strategy works

Every closed candle is checked for a Supertrend flip, confirmed by trend (EMA 50) and momentum (RSI).

1

Candle Close Scan

Each candle close across your configured watchlist triggers a single-flight scan against the Binance futures API.

2

Filtered Trend Entry

When Supertrend flips with the close on the same side of EMA 50 and RSI between 50 and 80 (long) or 20 and 50 (short), a limit entry is set at the low or high of that candle.

3

Automated Lifecycle

Positions track fill confirmation, fixed 2.0% TP and 1.0% SL after fees (a true 1:2), and max holding periods, with instant alerts sent via Telegram.

Specifications

Standard strategy parameters

Default parameters used across all scanner iterations on Binance USDⓈ-M futures.

Parameter Setting Description
Supertrend ATR 10 × 3 A flip up is a LONG candidate, a flip down a SHORT candidate.
Trend Filter EMA 50 The candle must close above it for a LONG, below it for a SHORT.
RSI Filter (14) 50–80 / 20–50 LONG needs RSI above 50 and below 80, SHORT below 50 and above 20.
Limit Entry Low / High Set at the signal candle's low for a LONG, high for a SHORT.
Take Profit (TP) +2.0% Net gain after fees; the target sits the fee further out (2.1%).
Stop Loss (SL) -1.0% Net loss after fees, 1R; the stop sits the fee closer in (0.9%).
Estimated Fee 0.10% Deducted on round-trip trades when calculating net performance.
